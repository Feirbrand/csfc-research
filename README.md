# CSFC Research — Complete Symbolic Fracture Cascade

**A substantial public research library for the protected CSFC project, by Aaron M. Slusher.**

**What this repository is for:** CSFC has more public explanatory substance than a single paper or an index entry. This repository brings together the already-published six-part public series in readable full-text form, preserves explicit links to the original files, and provides a guide to CSFC's historical v1 framework, its observations, limitations, and research relationships. It is a standalone knowledge home, not an invitation to reproduce the protected engine.

**Status:** public research documentation; restricted-public / source-available, **not open-source CSFC implementation**. The canonical v1 paper remains at its published path in Synoetic OS, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17309239](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17309239). ### What the series does and does not establish

- **Published framework:** CSFC v1.0 is presented as a five-stage cascade: Data Fragmentation → Symbolic Identity Fracturing (SIF) → Symbolic Drift Cascade (SDC) → Role Obsolescence Cascade (ROC) → Complete Collapse. The Vulnerability Window is described separately as a transition/partial-recovery condition, not a newly inserted sixth stage.
- **Historical inconsistencies:** Parts 2–6 used inconsistent stage subtitles and some historical framing; Part 1 supplies the v1 stage sequence. The canonical current name is **Complete Symbolic Fracture Cascade**, and SRA refers to **Symbolic Role Architecture**. Preserve historical wording as historical text, not as a competing authoritative rename.
- **Evidence status:** The incident accounts and practitioner analogies are author-reported observations and interpretations, not independent validation of predictive accuracy, neuroscience equivalence or recovery percentages. Readers should distinguish examples, hypotheses, external studies and demonstrated results.
- **Human/AI connections:** CSFC is discussed alongside Neuroformation, NFA/ASFA, Elevation Grid and the broader adaptive-cognition research program as a cross-domain relationship. Such comparisons do not prove identical human neurological and AI computational mechanisms.
- **Protected boundary:** No unreleased detector internals, production thresholds, recovery routing, private incident logs, private v2/v3 details or linked proprietary Torque/UTME/PME mechanisms are licensed or added here. Publication of explanatory documents does not grant access to their protected implementations.
